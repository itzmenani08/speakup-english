Word of the Day

Meaning: మెరుగుపరచడం

📝 Basic Grammar

Simple Present Tense

Used for daily habits and regular actions.


Example:
I go to college every day.

Simple Past Tense

Used for completed actions in the past.


Example:
I watched a movie yesterday.

Simple Future Tense

Used for future actions.


Example:
I will learn English.
